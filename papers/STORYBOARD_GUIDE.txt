================================================================
 LSPU SCHOLARHUB - STORYBOARD SCREENSHOT GUIDE
 (Follows the APPENDICES sample format: each screenshot gets a
  caption that DESCRIBES WHAT THE PAGE SHOWS/DOES)
================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE
---------------------
1. Take the screenshots in the order below, role by role.
2. For each shot: follow "HOW TO GET THERE", set up the page using
   "BEFORE YOU CAPTURE", then screenshot.
3. Under each screenshot in your document, paste the caption from
   "CAPTION (paste this)".
4. Number the figures continuously (Figure 1, Figure 2, ...).

GENERAL TIPS
------------
- Use ONE browser for all screenshots (consistent look).
- Window size: maximize the browser. Do NOT zoom in or out (100%).
- Always show the top navigation bar and a bit of the page content.
- Guest shots: use an Incognito / Private window (must be logged out).
- Log in order: Guest shots first -> Student -> Staff -> Admin ->
  Super Admin. (You can also fully do one role at a time.)
- Test accounts: use ONLY the approved test accounts
  (Staff: Alfonso; Students: the 3 test student accounts;
  Super Admin: superadmin / lspu.scholarhub@gmail.com).
- Make sure the pages SHOW DATA (applications, students, programs)
  before capturing - storyboards look dead with empty tables.
- Hide/crop any real personal emails or numbers that are not test data.

LEGEND
------
[HOW]  = steps to reach the page
[SETUP]= what to click/prepare so the shot looks complete
[CAPTION] = the descriptive sentence to paste under the figure

================================================================
 ROLE 1: GUEST  (take these in an Incognito window, logged out)
================================================================

G1 - LANDING PAGE
[HOW] Open the site URL. You land here automatically while logged out.
[SETUP] Scroll to show the hero section AND the navigation bar.
[CAPTION] Landing page of LSPU ScholarHub showing the scholarship
office information, program overview, and options to log in or
register.

G2 - SCHOLARSHIP LIST (PUBLIC)
[HOW] From the landing page, click the Scholarships / Programs link
in the navigation.
[SETUP] Make sure at least 2-3 visible (not hidden) programs show,
with their status badges.
[CAPTION] Public scholarship program list where guests can browse
available programs with their descriptions, benefits, and open or
closed status before signing up.

G3 - REGISTRATION PAGE
[HOW] Click Register on the landing page.
[SETUP] Show the form with the upload areas for the Certificate of
Registration (COR) and Report of Grades (ROG), the "I am already a
scholar in LSPU" option, and the fields that get auto-filled by OCR.
Do NOT submit.
[CAPTION] Student registration page where the applicant uploads the
COR and ROG, and the system automatically reads the student details
from the files.

G4 - LOGIN PAGE
[HOW] Click Login in the navigation.
[SETUP] Show the empty login form.
[CAPTION] Login page where users enter their credentials and are
redirected to the dashboard for their role.

================================================================
 ROLE 2: STUDENT  (log in using a test STUDENT account)
================================================================

S1 - STUDENT DASHBOARD
[HOW] Log in as a student with an existing application.
[SETUP] The stat cards should show real numbers (total applications,
pending, approved) and the reminder to keep COR and grades updated.
[CAPTION] Student dashboard displaying application summary, pending
and approved counts, and the reminder to keep registration documents
and grades updated.

S2 - BROWSE SCHOLARSHIPS (STUDENT VIEW)
[HOW] Click Scholarships in the navigation.
[SETUP] Show open programs with the Apply button visible. If the
student already has a pending public application, the applied
program can show its applied state instead.
[CAPTION] Scholarship list for students showing programs available
to their campus and academic period, with the option to apply.

S3 - APPLICATION FORM
[HOW] Click Apply on one open program.
[SETUP] Show the form pre-filled with the student's details
(birthday, course, GWA, units) plus the program's custom questions.
Do NOT submit.
[CAPTION] Scholarship application form with the student's details
pre-filled from their profile and registration files.

S4 - MY APPLICATIONS
[HOW] Submit the application from S3 (or use an existing one), then
open My Applications.
[SETUP] The list should show the application with its status
(Submitted / Under Review) and the progress indicator.
[CAPTION] My Applications page listing the student's submissions
with their current status and review progress.

S5 - APPLICATION DETAILS
[HOW] On My Applications, click View on one application.
[SETUP] Show the progress tracker (Submitted -> Under Review ->
Decision) and any review notes.
[CAPTION] Application details showing the progress tracker from
submission to decision, including notes from the scholarship office.

S6 - MY SCHOLARSHIP
[HOW] Open My Scholarship from the navigation.
[SETUP] This page needs the student to have an APPROVED application.
If your test student has none, approve one application as staff
first (see ST4), then come back to this shot.
[CAPTION] My Scholarship page showing the details of the approved
scholarship program, including benefits, award amount, and batch
assignment.

S7 - NOTIFICATIONS
[HOW] Open Notifications from the navigation.
[SETUP] There should be at least one announcement (send one as staff
if needed, see ST7).
[CAPTION] Notifications page showing announcements sent by the
scholarship office for the current academic period.

S8 - MY PROFILE
[HOW] Open My Profile.
[SETUP] Show the locked fields (name, student number, campus,
course, year level) with their "contact the scholarship office"
notes, and the editable email and phone fields.
[CAPTION] Student profile page showing personal information; the
name, student number, campus, course, and year level are locked and
updated only through the scholarship office.

S9 - DOCUMENTS (COR / ROG UPLOAD)
[HOW] Open the Documents page.
[SETUP] Show the two document cards (COR and ROG) with their
upload/replace buttons and the PDF-only note.
[CAPTION] Documents page where the student uploads or replaces the
Certificate of Registration and Report of Grades, which the system
validates on upload.

S10 - AI CHATBOT
[HOW] Open the AI assistant from the navigation.
[SETUP] Ask it a real question (e.g. "What scholarships can I apply
for?") so the answer is visible, then capture.
[CAPTION] AI assistant answering a scholarship question using live
data from the system.

================================================================
 ROLE 3: STAFF  (log in using the Alfonso STAFF test account)
================================================================

ST1 - STAFF DASHBOARD
[HOW] Log in as staff.
[SETUP] Stat cards should show real counts for the campus and
current period.
[CAPTION] Staff dashboard showing application counts, pending
reviews, and approved scholars for the assigned campus and academic
period.

ST2 - MANAGE SCHOLARSHIPS
[HOW] Open Scholarships / Manage Scholarships.
[SETUP] Show the program list with status badges and the action
buttons (view, close, hide).
[CAPTION] Scholarship program management page where staff maintain
programs, their status, and visibility.

ST3 - CREATE SCHOLARSHIP FORM
[HOW] Click Add New Program.
[SETUP] Show the full form: name, description, funding type
(public/private), campus, academic period dropdown, maximum
applicants, and the custom fields builder. Do NOT save.
[CAPTION] Scholarship program creation form with funding type,
campus, academic period, applicant limit, and custom application
fields.

ST4 - REVIEW APPLICATIONS
[HOW] Open Review / Applications.
[SETUP] Open one pending application so the student details, GWA,
and notes area are visible.
[CAPTION] Application review page showing the applicant's details
and grades, where staff approve or reject with notes.

ST5 - BATCHES
[HOW] Open Batches.
[SETUP] Show an existing batch for a program with its approved
students, or the batch creation view.
[CAPTION] Batch management page where approved applicants are
grouped per scholarship program and exported to Excel.

ST6 - STUDENT DIRECTORY
[HOW] Open Students.
[SETUP] Show the list with a search/filter applied so it looks
intentional.
[CAPTION] Student directory listing registered students with their
profiles, documents, and application status.

ST7 - SEND ANNOUNCEMENT
[HOW] Open Announcements / Notifications.
[SETUP] Open the compose modal so recipient options (all students,
by scholarship, by batch, individual) and the optional attachment
field are visible. Do NOT send.
[CAPTION] Announcement composer with recipient selection and
optional file attachment, delivered through email and SMS.

ST8 - REPORTS
[HOW] Open Reports.
[SETUP] Charts need data - use the current period with the most
applications.
[CAPTION] Reports page with charts on applications, approvals, and
GWA distribution for the selected period and campus.

ST9 - MONITOR SCHOLARS
[HOW] Open Monitoring.
[SETUP] Show a scholar list and, if available, a submitted
Monitoring and Evaluation form entry.
[CAPTION] Scholar monitoring page tracking active scholars and
their Monitoring and Evaluation form submissions.

================================================================
 ROLE 4: ADMIN  (log in using your ADMIN test account)
================================================================
 NOTE: Admin pages mirror the staff pages, so A3, A7, A8, A9 look
 like ST2, ST8, ST6, ST7 but with the admin menu. Capture them
 anyway - the thesis needs the admin set complete.

A1 - ADMIN DASHBOARD
[HOW] Log in as admin.
[SETUP] Show the campus selector and academic period selector in
the header with real stats below.
[CAPTION] Admin dashboard with campus and academic period selectors
that scope all displayed data.

A2 - MANAGE USERS
[HOW] Open Manage Users.
[SETUP] Show the user list with the role filter, and the actions
menu (change role, assign campuses) visible on one row.
[CAPTION] User management page where administrators create accounts,
change roles, and assign campuses.

A3 - MANAGE SCHOLARSHIPS
[HOW] Open Scholarships.
[SETUP] Same setup as ST2.
[CAPTION] Scholarship management as handled by the campus
administrator, with the same program controls available to staff.

A4 - MANAGE CAMPUSES
[HOW] Open Manage > Campuses in the navigation.
[SETUP] Show the campus list with names, short names, status badges,
and user counts. If possible, show the Add Campus button.
[CAPTION] Campus management page where the administrator adds,
edits, activates or deactivates campuses used across the system.

A5 - ACADEMIC PERIODS
[SETUP] Show the campus list with codes.
[CAPTION] Campus management page maintaining the university campuses
used for registration and data scoping.

A5 - ACADEMIC PERIODS
[HOW] Open Periods.
[SETUP] Show the period list, with the current period marked, plus
the create/delete controls.
[CAPTION] Academic period management where administrators create,
delete, and set the current period that scopes system data.

A6 - AUDIT LOG
[HOW] Open Audit Log.
[SETUP] Apply the Category and Role filters so they are visible in
the shot.
[CAPTION] Audit log with category and role filters recording every
significant action performed in the system.

A7 - REPORTS
[HOW] Open Reports. Same as ST8.
[CAPTION] Campus analytics reporting for the administrator.

A8 - STUDENT DIRECTORY
[HOW] Open Students. Same as ST6.
[CAPTION] Student directory as managed by the administrator.

A9 - SEND ANNOUNCEMENT
[HOW] Open Announcements. Same as ST7.
[CAPTION] Announcement composer available to the administrator.

================================================================
 ROLE 5: SUPER ADMIN  (log in as superadmin)
================================================================

SA1 - SUPER ADMIN DASHBOARD
[HOW] Log in as superadmin.
[SETUP] Show university-wide stats and the campus selector.
[CAPTION] Super admin dashboard showing university-wide statistics
with the campus selector.

SA2 - MANAGE USERS
[HOW] Open Manage Users.
[SETUP] Show a multi-campus user (a user assigned to more than one
campus) or the role options (Super Admin, Admin, Staff, Student).
[CAPTION] System-wide user management with role assignment and
multi-campus access.

SA3 - MANAGE CAMPUSES
[HOW] Open Manage > Campuses in the navigation.
[SETUP] Show ALL campuses of the university with their names, short
names, status, and user counts.
[CAPTION] University-wide campus management listing every campus in
the system, with create, edit, activate/deactivate, and delete controls.

SA4 - ACADEMIC PERIODS
[HOW] Open Periods.
[SETUP] Show the periods with the current one marked.
[CAPTION] University-wide academic period management used to scope
all system data.

SA5 - AUDIT LOG
[HOW] Open Audit Log.
[SETUP] Show entries from multiple roles (use the Role filter
briefly, then clear it).
[CAPTION] Complete system audit trail covering all campuses and
user roles.

SA6 - REPORTS
[HOW] Open Reports.
[SETUP] Show the aggregated university-wide view.
[CAPTION] University-wide reports with per-campus or aggregated
views.

SA7 - CAMPUS SWITCHING
[HOW] Click the campus selector in the header.
[SETUP] Capture with the dropdown OPEN so the campus list shows.
[CAPTION] Campus selector in the header that switches the data
context for every page.

SA8 - SYSTEM SETTINGS / PERIODS
[HOW] Open the settings or periods page.
[SETUP] Show the global configuration options.
[CAPTION] System settings page for global configuration such as
academic periods and notification preferences.
