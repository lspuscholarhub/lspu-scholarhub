SUBJECT : 2026 REVISED GUIDELINES ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE TULONG DUNONG PROGRAM (TDP)

RATIONALE

These Revised Standardized Guidelines are issued in order to:

1. Ensure that appropriations for Tulong Dunong Program (TDP) are properly administered and implemented according to its purpose;
2. Establish uniform set of rules for the implementation of TDP under Republic Act (RA) No. 10931, or the Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education (UAQTE) Act, for all higher education institutions (HEIs);
3. Reflect the necessary amendments to TDP policies as approved by the UniFAST Board; and
4. Facilitate and expedite the validation and disbursement of grants to new and continuing TDP grantees for the 1st Semester of Academic Year (AY) 2026-2027 and onwards, thereby addressing the bottlenecks in the operations in the CHED Regional Offices (CHEDROS).

SECTION 1. COVERAGE

These Guidelines shall cover all State Universities and Colleges (SUCs), CHED-Recognized Local Universities and Colleges (LUCs), and Private Higher Education Institutions (PHEIs) that have Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with CHED-UniFAST duly executed by the head of institution/campus, provided, that all program offerings are covered by Certificates of Program Compliance (COPCs), or at least a Report on the Result of Preliminary Assessment (RRPA) for new program offerings for SUCs and CHED-Recognized LUCs with Institutional Recognition (IR), and Government Recognition (GR) for PHEIs.

SECTION 2. QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

An applicant for this grant must be a Filipino citizen and may be classified as any one of the following:

2.1 NEW TDP APPLICANT. NEW TDP applicants must be enrolled in any first undergraduate degree in SUCs, CHED-Recognized LUCs, or PHEIs that are in the CHED Registry of Programs and Institutions, under the existing MOA with CHED-UniFAST.

2.2 NEW TDP GRANTEE FOR 1st SEMESTER AY 2026-2027. These grantees shall be entitled to receive TDP benefits for 1st Semester AY 2026-2027 only. However,

for these grantees to be considered as continuing grantees, a similar funding shall be provided in the next Fiscal Year (FY) and succeeding years thereafter, until the grantee completes or graduates from the enrolled program.

2.3 CONTINUING TDP GRANTEE. A continuing grantee must be a TDP beneficiary in the semester or academic year prior to AY 2026-2027 with a corresponding TDP Award Number, and is enrolled in the current semester of an academic year, in any SUCs, CHED-Recognized LUCs, or PHEIs that are in the CHED Registry of Programs and Institutions, under the existing MOA with CHED-UniFAST.

SECTION 3. TDP GRANT AMOUNT

Continuing grantees shall receive the amount of Seven Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PhP7,500.00) per semester or Fifteen Thousand Pesos (PhP15,000.00) per academic year.

A new TDP grantee for AY 2026-2027 shall receive the amount of Seven Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PhP7,500.00) per semester or Fifteen Thousand Pesos (PhP15,000.00) per academic year, which may be increased as determined and approved by the UniFAST Board.

SECTION 4. GROUNDS FOR INELIGIBILITY OF NEW APPLICANTS AND DISQUALIFICATION OR TERMINATION OF CONTINUING TDP GRANTEES

4.1 Failure to meet the admission and retention policies of the HEI where the grantee is enrolled;
4.2 Failure to enroll for one (1) academic term with no Leave of Absence (LOA);
4.3 Failure to enroll for at least two (2) academic terms within an academic year;
4.4 Failure to submit information to the concerned CHEDRO on dropping out from school, deferment of the grant, or transferring to another program or HEI;
4.5 Suspension of grantee by his/her HEI for one (1) academic term or more;
4.6 Expulsion of the grantee from the HEI;
4.7 Failure to complete their bachelor's or comparable undergraduate degree a year after the period prescribed in their program pursuant to the Maximum Residency Rule (MRR);
4.8 Grantee who have been found to have submitted spurious document/s; and
4.9 Availment of multiple national government-funded financial assistance programs, except for Free Higher Education (FHE) in SUCs and LUCs under RA No. 10931, or the UAQTE Act. Further, as provided by the UniFAST Board, any student who is a recipient of any one (1)-time grant or subsidy, such as Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS) of the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), Student Monetary Assistance for Recovery and Transition (SMART), or the like, shall be eligible to apply for the TDP or shall not be delisted as continuing TDP grantee, provided that continuing grantees shall not receive TDP and SMART grants on the same semester. In no way shall the grantee receive both TDP grant and SMART grant at the same semester. Continuing TDP grantees shall continue to receive his/her TDP grant in the succeeding semester up to the allowed duration pursuant to the MRR.

SECTION 5. APPLICATION PROCEDURES

5.1 APPLICATION FOR NEW TDP GRANT. The UniFAST Secretariat will issue a call for submission of enrolled applicants together with documentary requirements after the enrollment period of the HEI, as evidenced by the submitted academic calendar.

Applications and documentary requirements shall be submitted either through partner implementers or directly to the concerned CHEDRO.

Continuing TDP grantees, except those who transferred to other HEIs, shall not be required to re-apply, but shall be validated and verified under Section 6.2 of these Guidelines.

5.2 UPLOADING IN THE PORTAL. The CHEDRO shall upload the submitted TDP applications in the Regional Coordinator (RC) Portal.

5.3 ELIGIBILITY CROSS-MATCHING. The CHEDRO and UniFAST Secretariat will conduct the checking of duplications, such as cross-referencing applicants with the database to prevent multiple grants being awarded to the same individual.

A list of qualified applicants is then finalized, and the results are generated through the RC Portal, and shall be released by the CHEDRO to the HEI under its jurisdiction.

5.4 SUBMISSION OF DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS TO CHEDRO. The HEI shall submit the required documents to the CHEDRO. In cases where an applicant fails to submit the required documentary requirements, the said applicant may directly submit the documents to their respective CHEDRO.

SECTION 6. VALIDATION AND VERIFICATION

6.1 VALIDATION OF NEW TDP APPLICANTS. The CHEDRO will validate the submitted documentary requirements and verify the student enrollment status. A Master List of Grantees will be issued to the respective HEIs for billing purposes. In lieu of the requirement for a maximum load per semester, the MRR shall be strictly imposed.

6.2 VALIDATION OF CONTINUING TDP GRANTEES. Ongoing or Continuing TDP grantees shall be validated by the CHEDRO using the Certificate of Registration/Enrollment (PDF copy) or Enrollment List. In lieu of the requirement of a maximum load per semester, the MRR shall be strictly imposed.

6.3 RETURNING STUDENTS. For CONTINUING TDP grantees who return to school after failing to enroll for one (1) academic term but have submitted their respective LOA, said grantees shall submit the certified electronically-generated Certificate of Registration/Enrollment as proof of enrollment and a certified copy of their approved LOAs.

6.4 STUDENT TRANSFEREES. For CONTINUING TDP grantees who are transferees from other HEIs, said grantees shall submit the certified electronically-generated Certificate of Registration/Enrollment as proof of enrollment. Continuing TDP grantees shall only transfer to any SUC, CHED-Recognized LUC, or PHEI that are in the CHED Registry of Programs and Institutions.

I. BILLING

CHEDRO processes the documents submitted by the HEI or grantee during the validation process, which will serve as the required documentation for billing and disbursement.

8.1 Only grantees included in the Master List shall be eligible for payment. The following forms shall be used:

1. Annex 2 (Forms 1, 2, and 3): Billing Forms for New/Continuing Grantees;
2. COR/COE (PDF copy) for new grantees; and
3. COR/COE (PDF copy) or EL (PDF copy) for continuing grantees.

B. Payment Through Check

1. Annex 2 (Forms 1, 2, and 3): Billing Forms for New/Continuing Grantees;
2. COR/COE (PDF copy) for new grantees;
3. COR/COE (PDF copy) or EL (PDF copy) for continuing grantees; and
4. ID of the student with a specimen signature, or in case of submission of a certificate of non-issuance of ID by the HEI, a copy of the same certification, and any government-issued ID with specimen signature (PDF copy).

C. Payment Through HEIs
Annex 2 (Forms 1, 2, and 3): Billing Forms for New/Continuing Grantees

8.2 The CHEDRO shall prepare the request for fund transfer (Annex A) corresponding to the billing documents for submission to the UniFAST Secretariat.

8.3 The CHEDRO requests a Sub-Allotment Release Order (SUB-ARO) and Notice of Transfer of Allocation (NTA) to the UniFAST Secretariat to distribute the allotments authorized by the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) through the Special Allotment Release Order (SARO).

8.4 Once CHEDRO receives the approved SUB-ARO and NTA from the UniFAST Secretariat, the CHEDRO shall prepare the Obligation Request and Status (ORS) and Disbursement Vouchers (DVs) in order to release the TDP benefits. The Management Fee shall be billed based on the TDP grants of the students.

II. DISBURSEMENT

A. Disbursement of grants to qualified and validated grantees shall be by direct payment from CHEDROS to grantees in the following order of priority:

1. Direct credit to the account of the grantees through LDDAP-ADA;
2. Check payments; and
3. In the event that (1) and (2) are not applicable, payments to grantees could be coursed through the HEIs.

All CHEDROS/HEIs must disburse the received benefits to students within **15 working days** upon receipt of the funds. CHEDROs/HEIs must submit a Certification on the completed disbursement of funds to the grantees within **seven (7) working days** after completion of disbursement (Annex 5).

B. In cases where the payment will be coursed through the HEIs:

(a) Any TDP grant not received by the grantee within **90 calendar days** upon receipt of the HEIs, such as in cases where the TDP grantee cannot be located, shall be returned to the CHEDRO for liquidation.

(b) Failure to disburse said funds will result in the non-release of succeeding Management Fee until such time that the funds are fully disbursed. The CHEDRO shall notify the concerned HEI on the withholding of the Management Fee. Repeated delay or non-release of TDP to grantees shall be a ground for termination of the MOA with the HEI as stated in *Section 11 of these Guidelines.*
(c) HEIs must maintain a separate bank account with the Land Bank of the Philippines (LBP) for proper account management of TDP.
(d) HEIs must issue official receipts for every amount received from the CHEDRO in relation to the implementation of TDP.
(e) HEIs must revert to the Cashier of the CHEDROs any excess fund transfers, if applicable.
(f) In the event that a TDP grantee is validated as eligible but unable to personally claim the grant, representative-claimants must submit the following documents to either the CHEDRO or the HEI where the funds have been transferred:

1. Copy of the validated grantee's ID;
2. Signed authorization letter from the grantee; and
3. Government-issued IDs of the claimant.

**For deceased grantees**, the following documents shall be submitted to either the CHEDRO or the HEI where the funds have been transferred:

1. Annex 6: Claim Form;
2. Death Certificate of the grantee; and,
3. Government-issued IDs of the claimant.

(g) All CHEDROs are enjoined to disburse funds to the HEIs using the Advice to Debit Account (ADA) under DBM Circular Letter No. 2018-14 dated December 28, 2018.

SECTION 9. MANAGEMENT FEE

A Management Fee of 1% will be applied to the total TDP amount for students enrolled in participating HEIs.

The Management Fee shall be divided equally between the HEI where the student is enrolled and the CHEDRO with jurisdiction over the HEI.

The Management Fee is allocated to cover administrative expenses related to the implementation of TDP. To ensure accurate disbursement and liquidation, HEIs must comply with the following requirements:

* **For Disbursement:** Submit the original copy of the Official Receipt (OR) upon receiving the TDP amount.
* **For Liquidation:** Provide a PDF copy of the OR.

SECTION 10. LIQUIDATION

If payment is coursed through the HEIs, SUCs, CHED-Recognized LUCs, and PHEIs must prepare and submit the required liquidation documents to the CHEDROs within **90 calendar days** from the receipt of funds to ensure proper reporting on the release and utilization of TDP funds:

A. Fund Utilization Report (FUR) (original copy);

**For SUCs and CHED-Recognized LUCs:**
The FUR must be duly certified by the Accountant and/or Vice President for Finance, duly received by the State Auditors of the Commission on Audit (COA), and approved by the President/Head.

**For PHEIs:**
The FUR must be duly certified by the Accountant and/or Vice President for Finance, verified or audited by a general administration and support unit other than the Internal Audit Service or Internal Audit Unit (or its equivalent), and approved by the President/Head.

B. OR of Management Fee (PDF copy); and

C. Proof of Grants Receipt using the following payment methods with reference to *Section 8(II) Disbursement.*

1. **Direct cash or check payments to grantees**
   * Original or Certified true copy of General Payroll or DV; and
   * ID of the student with a specimen signature, or any government-issued ID with specimen signature (PDF copy).

2. **Bank transfer**
   * Certified true copy of the DV issued to the disbursing bank;
   * Certified true copy of the Payroll Register/Payroll Proof List or any equivalent document confirming the transfer; and
   * Certified true copy of the OR with bank validation, or a certified true copy of the Letter of Instruction to the Branch Head/ADA with bank validation.

3. **Money remittance or Electronic wallet**
   Grantees prepare and submit the following documents to the HEI:
   * Letter request (email is also acceptable) from the grantee stating the preferred mode of payment/remittance including the account details (PDF copy); and
   * Electronic Confirmation Receipt or OR (PDF copy).

Any subsequent fund releases for SUCs, CHED-Recognized LUCs, and PHEIs shall be withheld pending the submission of the herein required liquidation report. Any unused funds should be returned to CHEDROs together with the liquidation report.

The liquidation of funds transferred to SUCs and CHED-Recognized LUCs shall be subject to the usual government accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

SECTION 11. SANCTIONS FOR VIOLATIONS OF THE GUIDELINES

After proper procedures were undertaken to conduct a fact-finding investigation of reported violations committed by any partner public or private HEI implementing the TDP, particularly concerning compliance with any provisions of these Guidelines, or related government accounting and audit regulations, the UniFAST Board, on the recommendation of the Executive Director, may impose any of the following sanctions, depending on the gravity of the violation:

1. Non-inclusion in the list of qualified HEIs to implement the TDP, without prejudice to any measures that may be undertaken by the UniFAST Board to maintain the TDP

availment of said HEI's qualified continuing TDP grantees;

2. Upon deliberation of the UniFAST Board, termination of the MOA for fraud, repeated non-liquidation of funds (failure to liquidate funds on at least two [2] semesters), and unjust non-release of TDP;

3. Recommendation to the CHED *En Banc* to impose particular sanction to the HEI for committing violations under RA No. 10931, after due process pursuant to RA No. 7722;

4. For serious violations that would merit the exercise of its authority, filing of criminal, civil, or administrative case against the erring official of the HEI, when warranted; and

5. Such other penalties or sanctions that may be approved by the UniFAST Board.

SECTION 12. INTERPRETATION CLAUSE

All doubts in the implementation, interpretation, and construction of the provisions of these Guidelines and the provisions of RA No. 10931 and its IRR relating to TDP shall be resolved in favor of a deserving TDP grantee and the optimized utilization of government resources.

SECTION 13. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE

Should any part of these Guidelines be declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other parts or provisions hereof not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 14. REPEALING CLAUSE

All other existing issuances, memorandum circulars, orders, and memoranda that are contrary to or inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Memorandum Circular shall be deemed repealed or modified accordingly. All other provisions not affected by this amendment shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 15. EFFECTIVITY

These Guidelines shall take effect starting the 1st Semester of AY 2026-2027.
